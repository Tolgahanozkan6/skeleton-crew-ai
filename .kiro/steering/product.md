# Product
Skeleton Crew AI helps small businesses triage requests, draft replies, and plan work. Category: Skeleton Crew. The starter is an offline demo; do not describe it as live AI. External actions require human approval.

The reusable skeleton supports two distinct domains: Support Desk for shop enquiries and Job Planner for service requests. Read docs/product-scope.md. Both currently have offline CLI starters; browser review, persistence and live AI are planned. Never claim contest readiness or actual Kiro use without evidence. Keep customer-facing outputs factual drafts; do not invent prices, bookings or refund approval.

# Requirements

1. When a non-empty request is submitted, the system shall produce triage, a reply draft, and operational tasks.
2. When input is empty or not a string, the system shall reject it.
3. Every result shall remain awaiting human review; the starter shall perform no external actions.
4. The demo shall run offline without credentials and identify itself as an offline demo.
5. Future live AI shall use a configurable provider adapter and structured output validation.
